Every site needs a first post, so here's mine.

## Why this site

I built this to have a place that's mine — not a platform, not a feed, not a profile.
Somewhere I can write at whatever length makes sense, link to things I care about, and
keep a record of the work I've done.

## What to expect

Mostly writing about software, design, and the intersection of the two. Some of it will
be technical. Some of it won't. None of it will be content-optimized for SEO.

If something here is useful to you, that's great. If not, that's fine too.
