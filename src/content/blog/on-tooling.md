Everyone has opinions about tools. Here are mine.

## The only rule

A tool should get out of your way. The moment you're thinking about the tool more than
the problem you're solving, it's costing you more than it's giving you.

## What I actually use

I try to keep the stack small and boring. Boring means well-documented, well-supported,
and unlikely to disappear. Small means fewer things that can go wrong, and fewer things
to learn when you come back to a project after three months away.

## On configuration

Deep configuration is a trap. You can spend an entire afternoon tuning your editor and
never write a line of code. The defaults are usually fine. Ship something.

---

The best tool is the one you already know.
